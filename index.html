About NCS

Professional Chimney Care
You Can Trust

With over 15 years of hands-on experience, Nick at NCS Services provides thorough, reliable chimney sweeping for homeowners across Daventry and the surrounding areas.

Whether you have a traditional open fireplace or a modern woodburning stove, Nick has the expertise and equipment to keep your chimney clean, safe, and fully operational.

What We Do

Our Services

Open Fires

Complete sweeping and inspection for traditional open fireplaces — removing soot, debris and blockages for safe, efficient burning all season long.

Wood Burning Stoves

Specialist sweeping for wood burning and multi-fuel stoves, ensuring your flue is clear and your stove is performing safely and efficiently.

Inspection & Report

A thorough inspection of your chimney and flue with a written report — giving you confidence and helping to meet home insurance requirements.
